The task.
The purpose of this task is to build an agentic dating site.

No questions will be answered. Work with what is written here, use your best judgment, and hand in your best output.

The main idea
Each person is represented by an agent. That agent dates on that person’s behalf. The agents date each other.

How it works
You find the people. Nobody hands you a list. Find at least 25 real people. Each person is two official links: their LinkedIn, and the Instagram that belongs to them. Only public Instagram profiles.
Each agent reads its person. Needs, hobbies, interests, and the other qualities it finds. This shows on a profile page we can open and look at.
The agents date on their behalf. Every person is represented by an agent. The agents date each other.
Each person gets a ranking. For every person, rank who fits them best.
The two sources
For every person, and for every agent, there are exactly two sources of information: the person’s LinkedIn and the person’s Instagram. Nothing else.
